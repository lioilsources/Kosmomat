Support

KosmoMat — an encyclopedia of the universe where every claim shows its source.

Found a factual error?

Please tell us which card, what is wrong and, ideally, a source. Every card has a public history; corrections are recorded with their reason and source. Write to business@olin.now.

How are cards labelled?

Each claim and theory carries two separate labels: strength of evidence and where the debate takes place. The rules are in the editorial constitution inside the app (Settings → Editorial constitution).

Does it work offline?

Yes. All content ships with the app; search works without a connection.

Podpora

  • Věcná chyba? Napiš, která karta, co je špatně a ideálně zdroj, na business@olin.now. Opravy se zapisují do veřejné historie karty.
  • Nálepky. Síla důkazů a místo debaty jsou dvě samostatné osy; pravidla najdeš v appce v Nastavení → Redakční ústava.
  • Offline. Celý obsah je v appce, hledání funguje bez připojení.
